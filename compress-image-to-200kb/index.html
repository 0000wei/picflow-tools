Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image to under 200KB?

Upload your image to PicEte and use the quality slider to find the right balance. For a 1920x1080 photo, 50-70% quality typically lands under 200KB. Smaller images like 800x600 can use 70-85% quality. The tool shows the output file size in real time, so adjust the slider until you hit the 200KB target.

Why do I need to compress images to 200KB?

200KB is a common file size limit for document uploads, scholarship applications, job portals, and some content management systems. Many online forms reject images larger than 200KB to reduce server load. Compressing your images ensures they'll be accepted while maintaining enough quality for most uses.

Will compressing to 200KB lose image quality?

Some quality loss is inevitable when compressing to 200KB, but PicEte uses smart JPEG compression that minimizes visible artifacts. At 200KB, most photos look excellent on screens and print well up to 5x7 inches. For critical applications like professional printing, consider whether a higher file size limit is available.

Can I batch compress images to 200KB?

Yes. Upload multiple images and the tool processes them all simultaneously. Since each image needs different compression levels to hit 200KB, you may need to adjust the quality slider for each one. Preview the results before downloading to ensure quality meets your needs.
