Por qué 1500x500 para Cabeceras de Twitter

Twitter recomienda oficialmente 1500x500 píxeles para las imágenes de cabecera de perfil. Este tamaño panorámico abarca todo el ancho de tu perfil en pantallas de ordenador y crea una apariencia profesional con marca. Preguntas frecuentes

What is the ideal Twitter header image size?

Twitter recommends 1500x500 pixels for profile header banners. This 3:1 aspect ratio displays correctly on both desktop and mobile. Keep in mind that Twitter crops the top and bottom on mobile, so avoid placing critical text or logos near the edges. The safe area for important content is approximately the center 600x200 pixels.

Why is my Twitter header cut off on mobile?

Twitter displays only the center portion of your header on mobile devices. The full 1500x500 banner is visible on desktop, but mobile shows a roughly 600x200 crop from the center. When designing your header, keep important elements like logos or text in the middle third of the image to ensure they're visible on all devices.

Can I use any image format for my Twitter header?

Twitter accepts JPG and PNG formats for header images. JPG is recommended for photos due to smaller file sizes, while PNG is better for graphics with text or logos. The maximum file size is 5MB. PicEte can convert your images to the optimal format while resizing to 1500x500, ensuring fast uploads and compatibility.

How often should I update my Twitter header?

Update your Twitter header whenever your branding changes, you have new promotions to announce, or you want to refresh your profile's appearance. Many businesses update headers seasonally or for special events. PicEte makes it quick to resize new banner images to 1500x500, so you can keep your profile fresh and engaging.
