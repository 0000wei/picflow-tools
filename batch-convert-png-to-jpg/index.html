Why Batch Convert PNG to JPG?

PNG files are lossless and uncompressed, making them 3-5x larger than equivalent JPGs. When you have hundreds of PNGs — from screenshots, design exports, or photo archives — converting to JPG can free up massive storage space. Frequently Asked Questions

How many PNG files can I convert at once?

PicEte can handle batch conversions of 100+ images simultaneously. Each image processes independently in the browser, so you're limited only by your computer's memory. For very large batches (500+), consider converting in smaller groups to ensure stable performance. The tool shows progress as each image converts.

Will I lose quality converting PNG to JPG?

JPG is lossy, so some quality loss occurs during conversion. However, at 85-95% quality, the difference is imperceptible for most photos. JPG files are 70-90% smaller than equivalent PNGs, making the tradeoff worthwhile for storage and sharing. Convert PNG to JPG for photos and graphics without transparency needs.

Can I download converted images individually or all at once?

Both options are available. After batch conversion, you can preview each converted JPG and download specific files that meet your needs. Alternatively, download all converted images at once as a ZIP archive for convenient bulk saving. This flexibility is perfect when you need to review results before keeping them all.

What happens to transparent areas in PNG when converting to JPG?

JPG doesn't support transparency, so transparent pixels are filled with white (or you can choose another background color). If your PNGs have important transparency, consider keeping them as PNG or using tools that can composite them onto backgrounds first. For standard photos and opaque graphics, this isn't a concern.
