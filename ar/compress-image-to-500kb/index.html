How do I compress images to under 500KB?

Upload your image to PicEte and adjust the quality slider. For a 1920x1080 photo, 60-80% quality typically results in 300-500KB files. For smaller images like 1200x800, 70-85% quality works well. The tool displays the output file size in real time, so you can find the perfect balance between quality and file size.

Why 500KB for email attachments?

Email providers like Gmail and Outlook allow attachments up to 25MB total, but many corporate email systems limit messages to 10MB. When sending multiple photos, keeping each image under 500KB ensures you can attach 20+ photos without hitting limits. Recipients also appreciate faster downloads, especially on mobile connections.

Will 500KB compression affect print quality?

500KB files typically print well up to 8x10 inches at 150 DPI. For standard photo prints (4x6 or 5x7), 500KB compressed images look excellent. For large format prints or professional applications, you may want higher file sizes. At 500KB, most users can't distinguish the compressed image from the original when viewed normally.

Can I batch compress to 500KB?

Absolutely. Upload all your images at once and PicEte processes them simultaneously. Set the quality slider once, and all images will be compressed to approximately the same level. Since each image compresses differently, check individual file sizes before downloading to ensure they're all under 500KB.
