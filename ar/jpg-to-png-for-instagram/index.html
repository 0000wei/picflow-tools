الأسئلة الشائعة

Why convert JPG to PNG for Instagram?

PNG is a lossless format that preserves maximum image quality, making it ideal for Instagram where compression during upload can degrade JPGs further. PNG also supports transparency for overlays and logos. While PNG files are larger, Instagram accepts them up to 30MB. Use PNG for final edited images, graphics with text, or when you need transparency features.

Does Instagram accept PNG files?

Yes, Instagram fully supports PNG uploads for posts, stories, and reels. You can upload PNG files up to 30MB just like JPGs. Instagram converts images during upload regardless of format, but starting with high-quality lossless PNG gives you the best chance of maintaining clarity after Instagram's own compression is applied.

Is PNG better quality than JPG for Instagram?

PNG is technically lossless and preserves perfect quality, but the difference is minimal after Instagram's compression. For most photos, JPG at 85-95% quality looks identical to PNG and uploads faster due to smaller file sizes. Use PNG for graphics with sharp lines, text overlays, logos, or when you've already applied editing and want lossless export.
