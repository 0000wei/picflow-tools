Image compression is essential for anyone working with digital images, from web developers and bloggers to e-commerce store owners and social media managers. Large image files are the number one cause of slow-loading websites, which hurts user experience and search engine rankings. Our free online image compressor uses smart compression algorithms to significantly reduce file sizes — by an average of 50-80% — while preserving as much visual quality as possible. The intuitive quality slider lets you choose your preferred compression level: 80-90% for high quality suitable for printing, 70-80% for excellent web use, or 60-70% for maximum compression ideal for thumbnails and previews. All processing happens locally in your browser, meaning your images never leave your computer — a critical privacy feature for sensitive or confidential images. With batch compression support, you can compress dozens of images simultaneously and see real-time savings statistics showing exactly how much space you saved.
