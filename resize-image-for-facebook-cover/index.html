Facebook Cover Photo Dimensions Explained

Facebook displays cover photos differently depending on the device. On desktop, the full 851x315 pixel banner is visible. However, on mobile devices, Facebook shows only a cropped center portion — approximately 560x280 pixels. This means important content like logos, text, or faces should be positioned in the center third of your image to ensure they're visible on both platforms. The 2.7:1 aspect ratio (851x315) is the official recommendation, but always keep mobile cropping in mind during design.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended Facebook cover photo size?

Facebook recommends 851x315 pixels for cover photos on desktop. This 2.7:1 aspect ratio displays the full banner on computer screens. However, Facebook displays a cropped center portion on mobile devices. The safe zone for important content is approximately the center 560x280 pixels to ensure visibility across both desktop and mobile views.

Why does my Facebook cover look different on mobile?

Facebook shows a significantly cropped version of your cover photo on mobile — roughly 560x280 pixels from the center. On desktop, you see the full 851x315 width. When designing your cover, place logos, text, and faces in the center third of the image. Avoid putting important elements near the edges as they'll be cut off on mobile devices.

What file format should I use for Facebook cover photos?

Facebook accepts JPG and PNG formats for cover photos. JPG is recommended for photos and graphics with gradients because it produces smaller file sizes. Use PNG only if you need transparency (though Facebook doesn't support transparent covers). The maximum file size is 100KB, and PicEte can optimize your images to meet this requirement while resizing.

How often should I update my Facebook cover photo?

Update your Facebook cover photo seasonally, for special promotions, when rebranding, or to announce major events. Many businesses change covers monthly to keep profiles fresh. Consistent updates signal that your page is active and engaging. PicEte makes it quick to resize new cover images to the proper dimensions whenever you need a refresh.
