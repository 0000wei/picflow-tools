Resize Image to 1200x630

Perfect Social Media Link Preview Size

Need to resize an image to 1200x630 pixels? This is the recommended dimensions for Open Graph (OG) images — those preview cards that appear when your content is shared on Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, Slack, and other platforms. Getting this size right means your links look compelling in social feeds, driving more clicks and engagement. PicEte makes it easy to create properly-sized social cards in seconds.

Why 1200x630 for Social Cards?

The 1200x630 pixel dimension (1.91:1 aspect ratio) is the sweet spot for link previews across major social platforms. Facebook explicitly recommends this size for OG images to ensure your preview appears sharp and without cropping. Twitter displays large cards at this ratio for maximum visibility. LinkedIn, Slack, and Discord all follow similar conventions. Using the correct size prevents your images from being compressed, stretched, or cut off when shared — ensuring your branding and messaging come through clearly.

OG Image Best Practices

When creating social cards at 1200x630, keep important text and graphics away from the edges — platforms may crop on mobile devices. Use high-contrast colors and readable fonts (minimum 40px for headlines). Include your brand logo but don't let it dominate the frame. Test how your card looks on different platforms since each applies slight variations in sizing and compression.
