Why Split into 3x3 Grids?

The 3x3 grid format is massively popular on Instagram for creating visual impact. When followers visit your profile, they see 9 posts that together form one cohesive image — a panorama, artwork, or creative message. This format increases engagement as viewers tap through individual tiles to see details. Brands and influencers use 3x3 grids for product launches, announcements, and storytelling campaigns that stand out in crowded feeds.

How PicEte Creates Perfect 3x3 Splits

Upload any image — horizontal, vertical, or square — and PicEte automatically divides it into 9 equal parts. For non-square images, we center the crop and calculate tile dimensions to maintain aspect ratio. Each tile is exported at 1080x1080 pixels (Instagram's recommended size) with numbered filenames so you know the posting order. The grid lines align perfectly when posted sequentially, creating a seamless appearance on your profile grid.
