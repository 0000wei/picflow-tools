JPG to PNG Tips

JPG to PNG for Transparent Background

One of the most compelling reasons to convert JPG to PNG is gaining transparency support. JPG format does not support transparent backgrounds at all, which makes it unsuitable for logos, icons, and overlay graphics. PNG, on the other hand, supports alpha channel transparency, allowing you to place images on any colored background without ugly white boxes. When converting JPG to PNG for transparency purposes, you will need to remove the background first using an image editor — our converter preserves the image data exactly as provided, so any existing background remains. Use this conversion as the first step in creating transparent PNG assets for your design projects.

When to Use PNG Instead of JPG

Choose PNG over JPG whenever image quality cannot be compromised or when you need crisp, sharp edges. PNG is the superior format for screenshots, because it captures UI elements and text without the blurry artifacts that JPG compression introduces around sharp edges. It is also the recommended format for infographics, charts, diagrams, and any image that combines photographs with text overlays. For web graphics and logos that must look sharp at any size, PNG's lossless compression ensures that your brand assets always appear professional. However, for photographs where file size matters more than absolute quality, JPG remains the more practical choice.
