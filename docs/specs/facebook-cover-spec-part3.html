7.4 FAQ 内容（英文版，包含 JSON-LD FAQPage）

QuestionAnswer
Why does my Facebook cover get cropped on mobile?Facebook displays cover photos differently on desktop (wide, 820×312) and mobile (taller crop, 640×360). The mobile version crops about 90px from each side. Our safe zone tool shows you exactly what stays visible on both.
What is the best size for a Facebook cover photo?Upload at 1200×628 pixels for the best quality. Facebook recommends 820×312 for desktop display. Always keep important content (text, logos, faces) within the center 560×312 safe zone.
Will my profile picture cover part of the cover photo?Yes. On desktop, the profile picture overlaps the bottom-left corner of the cover photo. On mobile, it sits centered at the bottom. Our immersive preview shows these overlays so you can design around them.
Can I use this tool without signing up?Absolutely. PicEte runs entirely in your browser — no account, no uploads to our servers, no limits. Your image never leaves your device.
What file formats are supported?JPG, PNG, and WebP are supported for upload. You can download your cover as PNG or JPG.
Why is my Facebook cover blurry after uploading?Facebook compresses images larger than 100KB. Use the JPG format with quality around 80-85% to keep the file under 100KB while maintaining visual quality.
What is the Facebook cover photo safe zone?The safe zone (560×312 pixels centered in the 1200×628 canvas) is the area visible on both desktop and mobile devices. Content outside this area may be cropped on one or both platforms.

FAQ JSON-LD: 使用 @type: FAQPage，每个 Q/A 一对 mainEntity 里的 Question / Answer。

选择器用途

8.2 移动端适配（<768px）

  • .preview-toggles → flex-direction: column; （或紧凑行的 wrap）
  • .canvas-wrapper → 占满屏幕宽度
  • #main-canvas → 宽度 100vw（减去 padding）
  • .controls → flex-direction: column; align-items: stretch
  • .steps → grid-template-columns: 1fr（堆叠）

  • .preview-btn 默认背景 transparent，hover 时变 bg-secondary
  • .preview-btn.active 背景 primary-color
  • #main-canvas border-color 适应暗色
  • 教程/FAQ 区域的背景色使用 bg-secondary
