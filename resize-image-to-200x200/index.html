Frequently Asked Questions

Why use 200x200 for thumbnails?

200x200 pixels is ideal for small thumbnails because it's large enough to be recognizable but small enough to load quickly. Many websites use 200x200 for comment avatars, forum profile pictures, and product preview thumbnails. At this size, images remain clear even on high-DPI displays while keeping file sizes minimal.

Will my images be clear at 200x200?

Yes. 200x200 is sufficient for thumbnails and small avatars to be clearly visible. PicEte applies anti-aliasing during downscaling to maintain clarity. For photos, faces remain recognizable at this size. For logos and icons, text stays readable. If you need higher detail for zooming, consider 400x400 or 512x512 instead.

Can I batch create thumbnails at 200x200?

Absolutely. Upload all your images at once and PicEte will resize them all to 200x200 simultaneously. Each image processes in milliseconds, so you can quickly generate hundreds of thumbnails for a website gallery, forum user avatars, or product previews without waiting.

What's the difference between 200x200 and 250x250?

200x200 is 56% smaller in file size and resolution compared to 250x250. Use 200x200 when you need minimal file sizes and fast loading, such as for user comment avatars or database thumbnails. Use 250x250 when you need slightly more detail and clarity. Both sizes serve similar purposes — the choice depends on your specific use case.
