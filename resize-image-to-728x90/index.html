Why 728x90 is the Leaderboard Standard

The 728x90 leaderboard format has been the dominant horizontal banner size since the early days of display advertising. This wide, shallow banner (8:1 aspect ratio) spans across desktop and tablet screens, making it highly visible while remaining unobtrusive. It's an IAB standard size, meaning virtually every ad network, publisher platform, and ad server supports it. Google AdSense defaults to 728x90 for many banner placements, and it remains a top-performing format for brand awareness campaigns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 728x90 called the leaderboard ad size?

The 728x90 format is nicknamed 'leaderboard' because it spans across the top of web pages like a leaderboard in sports. This wide banner size was standardized by the Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB) in the early 2000s and remains one of the most common display ad formats. Its prominence at the top of pages makes it highly visible and effective for branding campaigns.

Where do 728x90 leaderboard ads appear?

728x90 leaderboard ads typically appear at the top of web pages, below the navigation bar but above the main content. They're also sometimes placed at the bottom of articles. This horizontal format works well in both desktop and tablet layouts. On mobile, leaderboards are often resized or replaced with smaller mobile-specific ad sizes like 320x50.

Will my images look professional at 728x90?

Yes. 728x90 provides enough width for clear text and recognizable graphics. PicEte's downscaling preserves sharpness and maintains visual quality. For best results, start with horizontal images that have at least a 8:1 aspect ratio. If your source is vertical, it will be letterboxed with black bars on the sides.
