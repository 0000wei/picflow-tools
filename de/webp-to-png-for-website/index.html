Universal Browser Compatibility

WebP offers great compression, but PNG is supported everywhere. When website compatibility matters more than file size, converting WebP zu PNG ensures your images display correctly on all browsers and devices. PicEte converts WebP zu PNG instantly, preserving quality and transparency for universal web compatibility.

Why Convert WebP zu PNG for Websites?

While modern browsers support WebP, older versions (especially Internet Explorer and Safari before 14) don't display WebP images at all. If your audience includes users with legacy browsers or you need maximum compatibility, PNG is the safe choice. PNG also works better with image editing software, CMS uploads, and print workflows. Converting to PNG future-proofs your images and ensures they appear exactly as intended regardless of the viewer's browser or device.

Browser Support Considerations

WebP support reached 95%+ browser coverage recently, but that missing 5% can matter for enterprise environments, developing regions, and specific user segments. Corporate IT departments often standardize on older browsers. Government and educational institutions may run legacy systems. When you can't control your audience's browser version, PNG eliminates compatibility risk. PicEte's conversion maintains full image quality including alpha channel transparency, so your graphics look sharp on every platform.

Häufig gestellte Fragen

Why would a website need WebP converted to PNG?

Not all platforms and CMS systems support WebP uploads. Convert WebP zu PNG when: your website's media library rejects WebP, you're uploading to an older CMS, your CDN doesn't serve WebP correctly, or your contributors use browsers that don't handle WebP uploads reliably.

Does converting WebP zu PNG preserve image quality for web use?

Yes, PNG is lossless so the conversion preserves every pixel. The trade-off is file size — PNG will be 3-5x larger than the original WebP. For web use, consider re-compressing with a tool like TinyPNG after conversion, or keep WebP format where your CMS supports it.

Does the conversion retain transparency for web graphics?

Yes, both WebP and PNG support alpha transparency. Logos, icons, and graphics with transparent backgrounds convert perfectly. The PNG output is ready for immediate use in your website's design assets and image directories.

Can I batch convert multiple WebP images for my website?

Yes, upload all your WebP assets at once. The tool batch processes them and preserves filenames with the .png extension for easy website integration — just download and upload to your CMS or copy to your assets folder.
