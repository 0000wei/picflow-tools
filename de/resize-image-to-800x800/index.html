Resize Image to 800x800

E-commerce Product Photo Standard

Need to resize product photos to 800x800 pixels? This is the recommended image size for major e-commerce platforms including Amazon, Shopify, eBay, and Etsy. Square product images at this resolution look professional on all devices, load quickly, and meet platform requirements. PicEte makes batch resizing your product photography fast and simple.

Why 800x800 for E-commerce?

Online marketplaces recommend square images between 800x800 and 1000x1000 pixels. At 800x800, your product photos are large enough for customers to see details and zoom in, yet small enough to maintain fast page load speeds — critical for conversion rates. This size works across desktop and mobile layouts, ensuring your products display consistently whether viewed on a phone or a 27-inch monitor. Many platforms also use this size for thumbnails and search results.

Platform-Specific Guidelines

Amazon accepts 800x800 as the minimum for zoom functionality, with 1000x1000 preferred. Shopify's product grid works perfectly with 800x800 squares, and eBay recommends this exact dimension for category browsing. Etsy's listing photos display best at 800x800 or higher. Resizing all your product images to this standard creates a unified, professional look across your entire online presence and simplifies inventory management when selling on multiple channels simultaneously.

Product Photography Best Practices

  • Use pure white backgrounds (RGB 255,255,255) for marketplace compliance
  • Leave adequate padding around the product to avoid cropping
  • Ensure the product fills 80-85% of the frame for maximum visibility
  • Maintain consistent lighting and angles across your catalog
  • Save as high-quality JPG to balance file size with image clarity

Häufig gestellte Fragen

When would I need 800×800 images?

800×800 is the standard for e-commerce product photos (Amazon, Shopify, eBay), avatar/profile images, square product thumbnails, listing images on marketplace platforms, and mobile app icons. It's large enough to show detail but small enough to load quickly.

Can I batch resize product photos to 800×800?

Yes, upload all your product images at once and resize them all to 800×800 in a single batch operation. Each image maintains its aspect ratio. Ideal for e-commerce sellers who need consistent image dimensions across their entire catalog.

Will 800×800 be sharp on Retina displays?

800×800 appears as 400×400 on 2x Retina screens (like iPhones with Retina displays). For true Retina sharpness at a 400×400 display size, 800×800 is perfect — double the pixel density for crisp rendering.
