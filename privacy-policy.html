Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024

1. Overview

PicEte ("we", "our", or "this service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, use, and protect your information when you use PicEte online image tools.

2. Information Collection

2.1 Image Processing

All image processing functions (format conversion, resizing, compression, etc.) on PicEte are performed locally in your browser. We do not upload your images to our servers, nor do we store, view, or share your images.

2.2 Technical Information

We may automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Access times and pages
  • Language preferences

3. Use of Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Improve and optimize our services
  • Analyze website usage and trends
  • Prevent technical issues and improve user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Cookies

PicEte may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Provide personalized content

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect the normal functioning of certain features.

5. Data Security

Since all image processing is done locally in your browser, your images always remain on your device. We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect the technical information we collect, preventing unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

6. Third-Party Services

PicEte may use third-party services (such as website analytics, CDN, etc.). These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their data practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users of all ages. Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. In the event of significant changes, we will post a notice on our website. We recommend regularly reviewing this policy to stay informed of any updates.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the following channels:

Email: privacy@picete.com
Website: https://picete.com
